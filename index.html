the brand heart

Every outfit has a story.

Born from a mother’s quest for clothing that respects childhood innocence and sensitive skin.

Chapter 01

Comfort comes first. Always.

Childhood happens in movements — cartwheels in the garden, running through puddles, and deep midday naps. We craft our garments with flat seams, organic cotton, and zero scratchy labels so your child feels only clouds on their skin.

"If a child doesn't feel free to move, it isn't true luxury."
Chapter 02

Handmade with love by Indian artisans.

Every motif, block print, and gotta border is brought to life by master craftspeople across Rajasthan and Gujarat. We preserve time-tested Indian textile heritage while reimagining cuts for modern, carefree play.

"Passed down through artisan hands into little wardrobes."
Chapter 03

Little moments become lifelong memories.

From their very first family Diwali to birthday morning pancakes, clothes carry emotional bookmarks of who they once were. Katha creates heirloom keepsakes designed to be treasured, worn, and lovingly passed down to younger siblings.

"Every little giggle, every little milestone has a story."
